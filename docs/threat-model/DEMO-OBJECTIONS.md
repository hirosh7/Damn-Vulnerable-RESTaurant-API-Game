# Demo — Audience Objections & Responses

---

**"I don't trust AI to write my security code."**
> "This isn't about replacing your engineers — it's giving them a starting point that's 80% there so they can focus on the 20% that needs human judgment. The alternative is starting from zero."

---

**"We already have a threat model tool."**
> "Perfect — that's the 'Bring Your Own Diagram' flow. Export it, and the pipeline picks up at the security requirements step. No rework, no lock-in."

---

**"Security teams are already overwhelmed."**
> "That's exactly the point. The bottleneck isn't knowledge — it's the manual intake process. This removes it entirely."

---

**"We can't have AI making security decisions."**
> "It isn't making decisions — it's doing the intake work. Every requirement and every countermeasure still goes through your team for review. What changes is how long that review queue is."

---

**"This only works for simple architectures."**
> "The richer the diagram, the more survey answers fire, and the more requirements you get. Complexity helps the pipeline, it doesn't break it."

---

**"How do we know the requirements are accurate?"**
> "Every answered survey question has a comment citing the specific threat, component, or code evidence that drove it. You can audit the reasoning for any requirement in the project."

---

**"What happens when the threat model changes?"**
> "Re-run the OTM generator, create a new versioned SDE project, and diff the requirements count. The pipeline is repeatable — that's the point."
